Projects & Work

01

Light-Tracking Turret

Designed and 3D printed a motorized turret that rotates 360 degrees horizontally and tilts up and down vertically. The tip houses a photoresistor that scans the room to find the brightest light source. The system uses stepper and servo motors controlled via Arduino to autonomously point toward the brightest spot in the room. Combines CAD design, FDM 3D printing, and embedded systems programming in one complete project.

02

Proximity-Triggered Self-Opening Box

Built an automated box that opens on its own when a hand is detected nearby. An ultrasonic sensor measures distance in real time; once an object enters the trigger range, it signals a servo motor to lift the lid. Wired and programmed the full system using Arduino — a rough but fully functional prototype, and my first end-to-end electronics and embedded coding project.

04

Shower Pipe Cover — 3D Printed

Designed and printed a custom cover for a pipe protruding from a shower wall, where moisture, heat, and water exposure are constant. Chose PETG over PLA for its higher temperature resistance. Used a gyroid infill pattern to minimize channels where water could accumulate, and reduced layer height to improve watertightness. Every print setting was chosen deliberately based on the environment the part would live in.
